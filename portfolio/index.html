01 — ABOUT

Two disciplines, one method.

I build full-stack web and app products — mostly with Python and Django — backed by coursework from Harvard's CS50 series, MIT Open Learning, and the University of Michigan. Databases are part of the toolkit, not the whole job: I design the schema when a project needs one, but the focus is shipping working apps end to end.

Outside of client work, I write and illustrate Chipku, a sci-fi book series following a squirrel bounty hunter across a desert frontier world. Both crafts come down to the same instinct: take something sprawling and give it a clear, working structure.
